## 4. One Emotional / Philosophical Angle

- “Consistency > intensity”

Like pages in a book, they add up.  It's a matter of style to work for long or short periods, but consistency - showing up time and time again - is the important part.  There is a martial arts saying, "fall down seven, get up eight".  It means we have to keep trying, keep showing up, keep doing the work.  If we make that a given, then we can use our creativity and tools and friends and our whole self to accomplish what we need

## 5. One Tactical / Practical Tip

Concrete actions builders could take.

**Protect your next work session before ending the current one.**

At the end of each build session, leave yourself a note:

- What's the next task?
- What's blocked?
- What's the first thing you'll do next time?

Future-you can start immediately instead of spending 20 minutes figuring out where you left off.
