The admin are technically a reward track in the Hackathon and as admins they have rules that they must follow and requirements that must be fulfilled in order to get the reward pool at the end of the hackathon.

Of those rules are stated in the Gimbal Lab proposal that funded the original piece of pie hackathon https://github.com/gimbalabs/gimbalabs-project-archive/blob/main/projects/active/piece-of-pie-hackathon/project.md


Admins are responsible for:

    Coordination
    Participant tracking
    Hackathon website + enrollment flow
    Final presentation Meeting and logistics

Verification handled via public presentations.
