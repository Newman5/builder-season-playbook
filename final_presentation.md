## Slide 3: What the Product Does

Show the user-facing problem and the product in plain language.

- **Who the user is**  
  Community organizers, educators, open source projects, DAOs, companies, or anyone who wants to help a group of people consistently build something meaningful over time.

- **What the user can do**  
  Launch their own Builder Season using a complete set of templates, guides, checklists, and operational playbooks instead of starting from scratch.

- **What value the user gets**  
  A proven framework distilled from real experience that helps participants stay engaged, build consistently, and ship useful outcomes—not just demo-day projects.

- **Where payment happens**  
  The Builder Season Playbook is purchased through Gumroad as a digital download, with optional consulting available for communities that want help launching their first season.
